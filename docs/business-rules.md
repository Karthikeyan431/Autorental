# Business rules

Rules 1–11 were confirmed in the original brief. Rules 12–18 come from the 12 November meeting and the zero-cost decision; they need the client's sign-off, but we build them now.

Each rule names where it is enforced and the tests that prove it. Test names are suggestions; keep a test for every line.

| # | Rule | Enforced in | Must-have tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rent is charged for **every calendar day**, Sundays and public holidays included. | Accrual job: no weekday or holiday logic. DB: `UNIQUE (agreement_id, rent_date)` on `rent_charges`. | 4 days at ₹300 including a Sunday gives 4 charges and ₹1,200; a holiday is charged; 1–30 Nov 2026 gives 30 charges; Feb 2028 gives 29; a re-run makes no duplicates; 3 days of downtime are filled. |
| 2 | A driver can have **only one active auto** at a time, and an auto only one active driver. | DB: partial unique indexes on `agreements(driver_id)` and `(vehicle_id)` where active; exclusion constraints on overlapping date ranges. Service: eligibility check. | A second activation is refused; a direct SQL insert of a second active agreement raises a DB error; overlapping ranges are refused. |
| 3 | The owner sets the **rent due time**; a change never alters past days. | `settings_versions` (insert-only); each charge stores its own `due_at` and `settings_version_id`. For a driver on the weekly rhythm, `due_at` is the first `weekly_pay_day` on or after the rent date, at the rent due time (N3, awaiting client confirmation). | Changing the due time today keeps existing charges' `due_at`; new charges use the new time; a weekly driver's mid-week charges are not overdue before the weekly pay day; changing a driver's rhythm never changes existing charges' `due_at`. |
| 4 | **Staff choose which days a payment covers.** A pay request or statement match only suggests days; a person confirms them. | Service: allocations are created only by an explicit confirm action, never automatically. Suggested days are recomputed from the driver's oldest unpaid days at confirm time, never copied from an old request. | A statement match creates suggested days but no allocation until confirmed; no code path allocates without a user action; you cannot allocate to another driver's day, to a future day (Q16), or beyond a day's balance or the payment's unallocated amount; a match against a superseded request suggests the driver's current oldest unpaid days. |
| 5 | The owner controls **advance adjustments**. | Advance transactions stay pending until the owner approves; a setting can switch adjustments off; refunds are off (Q5). | A pending adjustment does not change outstanding; an adjustment above the balance is refused; the balance never goes below 0. |
| 6 | **KYC comes before any auto.** | Eligibility check inside the activation transaction. | Activation is refused for every KYC state except approved. |
| 7 | **Identity and licence are separate checks**, each with its own status and history. | `kyc_checks` has one row per attempt per `check_type`; `kyc_events` holds history. | Approving identity does not approve licence; history keeps every attempt. |
| 8 | **Government (free) and manual verification are both supported.** | KYC methods: `uidai_qr`, `uidai_app_attested`, `parivahan_lookup`, `manual`. | Each method can complete a check; method and evidence are stored. The two UIDAI methods are behind flags (`AADHAAR_QR_INAPP_ENABLED`, `AADHAAR_APP_ATTEST_ENABLED`), both off by default until N13 is answered (D47); with both off, manual review plus the Parivahan lookup still completes KYC. |
| 9 | KYC starts **at registration** and shows **on the dashboard**. | Registration wizard; dashboard KYC counts. | A new driver appears in the KYC queue; dashboard counts match the queue. |
| 10 | Only **approved, eligible, active** drivers get an auto: identity approved, licence approved and valid on the date, account active, no blocking restriction. | `check_eligibility()` in the agreements service, called inside the activation transaction with row locks. | Each condition failing alone is refused with its reason code; a licence valid until yesterday is refused and until today is allowed. |
| 11 | **Important actions are recorded.** Mistakes are corrected with a visible reversal, never edited or deleted. | Insert-only `audit_logs` with a hash chain (the app DB role has no UPDATE or DELETE on it); payments, allocations and charges are never updated in place except status fields with audit. | UPDATE or DELETE on `audit_logs` fails; a reversal restores the day balance and writes an audit entry; the hash chain verifies; rejecting a payment marks its allocations reversed with the reason "payment rejected" and deletes nothing. |
| 12 | **Staff verify KYC**; the owner can reopen any verification. | Permission `kyc.decide` (staff default on, N2); `kyc.reopen` is owner-only. | Staff can approve; staff cannot reopen; an owner reopen creates a new attempt in `under_review` (the previous attempt and its history are kept) and re-checks eligibility for drafts. |
| 13 | **Every payment counts only after the owner approves it.** | Day balance counts only allocations of payments with `status = 'approved'`; receipts are issued at approval. | An unapproved payment never reduces outstanding or overdue; a rejected payment never counts; the receipt number is issued at approval; a driver awaiting approval is shown as overdue and flagged "payment awaiting approval" (N8). |
| 14 | Every driver has a **creator** and a **managing staff member**; only the owner changes the manager. | `drivers.created_by` (immutable) and `drivers.managed_by`; `driver_manager_history`. | `created_by` cannot change; staff cannot reassign; a reassignment writes history and moves open escalations. |
| 15 | The owner **escalates missed rent** to the managing staff member; the escalation stays open until the rent is cleared or the owner closes it. | Escalations service; one open escalation per driver (partial unique index). | Only the owner raises it; it is assigned to the current manager; it auto-resolves when the driver's overdue reaches 0; the owner can close it with a reason; a second open escalation for the same driver is refused. |
| 16 | **Up to 10 active staff accounts**; only the owner can raise the limit. | Setting `staff_limit` (default 10), checked on create and reactivate. | The 11th active staff member is refused; after the owner raises the limit, it is allowed. |
| 17 | **No paid outside service is used.** | Code review and dependency list; no gateway, SMS or paid-API client in the code. | `/review` checks new dependencies; the configuration has no paid-provider keys. |
| 18 | **Pay requests** start only for an eligible driver with an active agreement, and never ask for more than the rent due. | Pay request service. | No request for a driver without an active agreement; the amount equals outstanding due up to that date, never more; one request per driver per period (idempotent). |

## Rule-change policy

Changing any rule above needs the client's written approval. When one changes, update this file, `docs/open-questions.md`, the tests and the code in the same commit, and note it in the commit message.

Clarifications made on 12 November 2026 by the agency lead (rules 3, 4, 11 and 12 wording) are recorded in `docs/decisions.md`. The weekly-rhythm due date in rule 3 awaits client confirmation under N3.
